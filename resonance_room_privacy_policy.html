Privacy Policy

Effective Date: October 19, 2026

Resonance Room (“the App”) is a wellness audio application designed to provide relaxing sound experiences for rest, focus, and everyday well-being. We respect your privacy and aim to keep your experience simple, secure, and free from unnecessary data collection. This Privacy Policy explains how information is handled when you use Resonance Room on iOS or Android.

1. Information We Collect

Resonance Room does not require you to create an account or provide personal information to listen to its audio content.

The App is designed to operate primarily on your device. Certain information may nevertheless be processed by your device's operating system, the app store, or integrated third-party services. The information involved depends on the services included in the version of the App you use.

2. Information Stored on Your Device

Resonance Room may store information locally to support its functionality, including:

  • Your selected sound.
  • Playback preferences and playback state.
  • Sleep Timer settings.
  • Welcome screen completion status.
  • Information needed to display your Premium access status.

This information is used to provide a consistent listening experience. Locally stored app information may be deleted when you uninstall the App, subject to your device's backup and restoration settings.

3. In-App Purchases

Resonance Room offers an optional one-time purchase called Lifetime Access, which unlocks Premium sound experiences.

Purchases are processed through Apple App Store or Google Play, depending on your device. We do not directly collect or store your payment card number or other complete payment credentials.

Apple or Google may process purchase-related information according to their respective privacy policies. The App may access purchase status and transaction information provided by the relevant platform to verify purchases, unlock Premium features, and restore previous purchases.

For more information, see the Apple Privacy Policy and Google Privacy Policy.

4. Analytics, Diagnostics, and Third-Party Services

The App may use platform services or third-party software components necessary for functionality, purchase processing, and technical operation.

If analytics, crash reporting, or other third-party services are integrated, they may process technical information such as device characteristics, app version, crash details, or usage events, depending on their configuration.

Any such collection must be reflected in this Privacy Policy and the applicable app store privacy disclosures.

5. How Information Is Used

Information processed in connection with Resonance Room may be used to:

  • Provide audio playback and Sleep Timer functionality.
  • Remember local app preferences.
  • Verify and restore purchases.
  • Maintain application functionality and security.
  • Diagnose technical issues where diagnostic services are enabled.
  • Respond to support requests you voluntarily submit.

We do not require an account to access the App's basic functionality.

6. Information Sharing

Resonance Room uses Apple App Store and Google Play services to process and verify purchases. Relevant information may be processed by those providers as necessary to complete transactions and maintain purchase entitlements.

If you contact support, the information you choose to provide may be used to respond to your inquiry. Any additional sharing associated with third-party SDKs must be disclosed according to the services actually integrated into the App.

7. Data Security

We aim to minimize the information required to operate Resonance Room. Information stored locally is subject to the security protections available through your device and operating system.

Purchase processing is handled through the applicable app store's payment infrastructure. No method of electronic storage or transmission can be guaranteed to be completely secure.

8. Data Retention and Deletion

Local app settings are generally retained on your device until they are removed, reset, or deleted through the App or operating system.

You can remove locally stored app information by uninstalling Resonance Room, subject to applicable device backup and restoration behavior.

Purchase records are maintained by Apple or Google according to their respective policies and applicable legal obligations.

If you contact support, information included in your correspondence may be retained as reasonably necessary to respond to your request and meet applicable obligations.

For questions about personal information or deletion requests, please contact us using the contact details below.

9. Children's Privacy

Resonance Room is a general wellness and audio listening application and is not specifically directed toward children.

We do not intentionally request personal information from children through an in-app account registration process. If you believe a child has provided personal information directly to us, please contact us so we can review the request and take appropriate action.

10. International Users

Resonance Room may be available in multiple countries. Depending on your location, applicable privacy laws may provide rights regarding access to, correction of, or deletion of personal information.

You may contact us to submit a privacy-related request. We will review requests in accordance with applicable law.

11. Changes to This Privacy Policy

We may update this Privacy Policy when the App's features, third-party integrations, data handling practices, or legal requirements change. Any updated policy will be published with a revised effective date.

We encourage you to review this page periodically.
